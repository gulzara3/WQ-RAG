STATION: USGS 01646500 Potomac River near Washington, DC (Little Falls Pump Station)
REGION: Mid-Atlantic / East Coast. Drinking-water intake for Washington Aqueduct.
HYDROLOGY: Large regulated river; spring freshets; summer low flow; occasional tidal
influence downstream of the station.
LAND USE & STRESSORS: Urban and suburban; combined sewer overflows (CSOs) after storms
raise turbidity and depress DO; road-salt pulses raise SC in winter; summer algal
blooms raise daytime pH and DO.
BASELINE CHEMISTRY: SC 200-600 uS/cm; pH 7.5-8.5; DO 6-14 mg/L; T 1-30 C; turbidity 2-50 FNU.
TYPICAL ANOMALIES: storm turbidity spikes (> 100 FNU), CSO-driven DO sags, winter SC spikes.
REGULATORY CONTEXT: EPA NPDWR / Secondary Standards; DC DCMR Title 21 water-quality standards;
Safe Drinking Water Act source-water protection.
